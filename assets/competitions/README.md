These transparent PNG marks are displayed beneath each England fixture.

- `world-cup.png` is derived from FIFA's World Cup trophy artwork.
- `euros.png`, `european-qualifiers.png`, and `nations-league.png` are derived from UEFA's competition artwork.
- `friendly.png` and `other.png` are neutral football symbols because friendlies and uncategorised matches do not have a single competition identity.

All marks are rendered in the screen's warm ivory (`#F4F0E6`) so they remain legible on the oxblood background.
